## 1. Decision effect and boundary

This log reconciles the guide evidence in `UNIVERSITY_IDENTITY_EVIDENCE.md`, the source inventory in `IDENTITY_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`, the technical and rights findings in `IDENTITY_ASSET_AUDIT.md`, and the current `WEBSITE_VISUAL_IDENTITY_SPEC.md`. It is not a claim that Taibah University approved the project or the errata.

### 1.1 Binding precedence

Conflicts must be resolved in this order:

1. Explicit written rule in the official visual-identity guide.
2. Official supplied vector master, for the construction and geometry of the represented variant.
3. Consistent official production templates, as application evidence only.
4. Raster exports, for the exact supplied variant and observable technical properties only.
5. Project interpretation, including owner decisions, accessibility safeguards, and web fallbacks.

The first applicable higher source wins. A lower source may clarify but may not contradict or broaden a higher source. A guide example proves that the depicted treatment exists; it does not automatically create a universal rule, a public-use license, or permission for another language/configuration. Written guide color values override sampled asset and template colors. Microsoft Office theme colors and file metadata are not brand authority.

### 1.2 Decision labels and authority language

| Label | Binding meaning after reconciliation |
|---|---|
| **Direct** | Written or visibly specified by a cited guide page, or explicitly stated by a cited supplied license/source. A visual example must remain labelled as an example. |
| **Derived** | A necessary web or accessibility treatment that preserves Direct evidence without being attributed to the guide. |
| **Proposed** | A website-specific project decision. A locked owner decision is final for this project unless reopened, but is not an institutional mandate. |
| **Unresolved** | The supplied evidence cannot support the conclusion. The recorded conservative fallback controls until the named authority closes it. |

`Owner-authorized` means Faisal Albalwy authorized a project choice. `Gate G0 accepted` will mean the project workflow found the treatment compliant with its evidence and safeguards. Neither term means `institutionally cleared`, `university-approved`, `officially endorsed`, or legally licensed by Taibah University. Only separate evidence from the relevant rights holder or the Corporate Identity Unit can establish that.

6. **Space and size:** no official CSS formula or pixel minimum is claimed. BRD-006/008 may define and test project operational values; failure means enlarge, relocate, or omit the image mark.
7. **Color:** only the eight printed base HEX values are official. Accessibility governs semantic use. No sampled tint/shade is official, and no invented `approved darkened` state is allowed.
8. **Type:** Tosh A stays blocked; Alexandria Variable under the supplied OFL is the launch fallback, with license retention and later derivative QA.
9. **Patterns and icons:** style may inform later work, but supplied pattern/icon assets remain blocked from extraction or public derivative work until rights are evidenced. Their launch fallbacks are no branded pattern and text/separately licensed functional icons.
10. **Photography and templates:** no package photograph or template media is cleared. Launch may be photography-free. Templates inform rhythm only.
11. **Favicon/social:** no university symbol, logo-derived avatar/container, or improvised personal monogram. Social previews default to Faisal-first typography without the university logo.
12. **Privacy:** no raw source delivery; strip public-derivative metadata while retaining a private provenance record. Do not copy QR codes, template contacts, embedded people, or hidden document material.
13. **Project operations:** initial contact is institutional email only; third-party analytics are off. Nothing in identity templates overrides these controls.
