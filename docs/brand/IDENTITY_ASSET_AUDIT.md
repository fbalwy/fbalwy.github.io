# Taibah University identity asset audit

**Work package:** BRD-003 v1
**Audit date:** 18 August 2026 (Asia/Riyadh)
**Scope:** Read-only audit of `الدليل الارشادي للهوية البصرية.pdf` and all 40 files under `ملفات الهوية/`. No original was changed, copied to a public location, converted, or published.

## Decision boundary and evidence standard

This is an asset-suitability audit, not a grant of rights or a production-asset manifest. It reconciles with [the BRD-001 source register](IDENTITY_SOURCE_REGISTER.md): 1 guide plus 40 identity-package files, 20 identity-PDF pages, 4 rendered Office pages, and 10 presentation slides. All 40 package files are covered in the source map below.

`Observed` statements report facts seen in the guide, source register, native metadata, decoded files, and rendered visual samples. `Proposed treatment` is a later-work recommendation only. `Unresolved` means that the supplied evidence does not support a permission or a technical conclusion.

| Disposition | Meaning for this audit |
|---|---|
| Conditional ship | Eligible only as a documented candidate input to BRD-007 and subsequent Gate G0 review. It is not an approval to publish now. |
| Block | Do not embed, convert, serve, distribute, or expose publicly. |

| Unsuitable direct | Do not serve the supplied original. A later approved derivative is possible only where stated. |

### Rights boundary

- The owner authorization is limited to the unmodified official English horizontal logo PDF as an affiliation mark on this personal, English-only academic website. It supports this project decision; it does **not** prove Taibah University, logo, institutional, icon, template, photograph, or third-party clearance.
- The supplied official white English-horizontal PNG is a candidate dark-surface fallback in the website specification. Treat its use as conditional on the same institutional-rights note and visual approval; do not infer a broader logo license from the file.
- Alexandria has supplied SIL Open Font License 1.1 evidence. Tosh A has no supplied EULA or web-embedding, redistribution, conversion, or subsetting grant and remains blocked.
- A descriptive filename, a package location, Adobe metadata, a University Media Center author field, or the JPG title `Taibah University - Brand - Open Source` is not a license.

### Audit method and privacy check

All PDFs were rendered for visual review, including the two logo masters, all nine icon pages, the certificate/template PDFs, and guide logo/surface pages 18-23. Large pattern rasters were rendered at reduced scale for visual review. Native metadata was queried for creator, title, dates, colour, resolution, transparency, GPS fields, and Office author fields; Office packages were inspected for embedded media and comments/person parts. No queried GPS value was found, and no Office comments/person parts were found. This is not proof that future derivatives are privacy-safe: regenerated output must still be scanned and have nonessential metadata removed.

Observed metadata worth protecting from public delivery includes Adobe creation and modification timestamps and tools in PDFs, the document author/last-modified-by `Eisa Abdarahman Nafea Alharbai` in the A4 DOCX, and `المركز الاعلامي بجامعة طيبة` in the letterhead DOCX and presentation. Templates also visibly carry institutional contact details, QR/social marks, and embedded imagery. Raw Office/PDF templates must therefore remain non-public.

## BRD-003 conclusion and handoff risks

The only supported web candidates are (1) the owner-authorized complete English-horizontal vector logo, (2) its supplied white English-horizontal fallback after surface QA, (3) Alexandria under its supplied OFL notice, and (4) carefully cropped derivatives of the three pattern rasters after rights and visual review. No candidate is approved for public release by this audit. Tosh A is blocked. Icons, templates, certificates, bilingual/vertical logo variants, Office content, and embedded photography are reference-only or unsuitable direct sources unless a later evidence-backed decision changes their status.

Unresolved issues to carry into BRD-004/BRD-007/BRD-009:

1. Institutional/legal clearance for use of the owner-authorized affiliation treatment and the white fallback remains unproven by the supplied material.
2. Tosh A public-web rights remain unresolved; no derivative or embedding work may begin.
3. There is no per-icon or template/photo public-reuse clearance.
4. The guide does not specify a measurable digital logo clear-space module or pixel minimum; visual QA must establish a conservative implementation without inventing an official rule.
5. No supplied standalone mark, favicon, official SVG, English horizontal colour PNG, or public photograph may be assumed or fabricated.
