# CNT-001 v2 - evidence registry

**Status:** private claim-candidate registry; v2 remediation complete. No claim is approved for public copy or publication.

**Prepared:** 2026-08-19 (Asia/Riyadh)

**Scope:** protected English and Arabic CVs; 43 supplied local evidence files; and the five supplied live scholarly-profile endpoints. This registry implements the CNT-001 claim/source contract in `docs/CONTENT_GOVERNANCE.md`. It is an internal evidence register, not a public CV, catalogue, source archive, or content draft.

## 1. Non-negotiable handling rules

- Registration is not verification, eligibility, editorial approval, or publication. Every record begins fail-closed.
- `source_location` is represented only by a stable source ID and controlled-corpus alias. Private absolute paths, URL query state, document identifiers, signatures, QR/barcodes, phone numbers, addresses, student data, source-system URLs, and hidden metadata are deliberately omitted.
- Protected documents may support a minimal extracted fact, but are never cleared for redistribution or public download by this registry.
- English and Arabic CVs are owner-provided evidence in the same lineage. They do not independently converge on a claim. Platform records are source-specific; totals are never merged.
- `public_wording` and `public_location_ids` are `null` for every record. Only direct, fresh platform observations that satisfy all non-editorial axes may move from `hold` to `eligible`; no entry may move to `publish` without a named accountable person, category threshold, privacy/rights/consent checks, freshness, exact wording, and later editorial approval.

## 2. Schema and controlled defaults

Every individual claim represented by the ledger uses the full CNT-001 schema: immutable `claim_id`, version `1`, opaque `subject_id=SUBJECT-FA-001`, one controlled `category` and atomic `predicate`, typed source-form value, normalization method or `null`, `public_wording=null`, temporal fields, evidence language and translation state, citations, lineage IDs, threshold, verification/conflict/privacy/rights/consent/disposition fields, verification/observation/review timestamps, accountable/reviewer roles, supersession links, public locations, rationale, and append-only history.

Unless the row says otherwise, the controlled defaults are: `verification_status=collecting`; `conflict_state=none`; `privacy_class=Public_after_review`; `evidence_privacy_class=Restricted`; `rights_status=restricted`; `consent_status=not_applicable`; `public_disposition=hold`; `first_verified_at=null`; `last_verified_at=null`; `public_location_ids=[]`; and `record_owner_role=evidence_steward`. Every row has a single registration history event at `2026-08-19T00:49:27+03:00`.

Claim IDs use the immutable pattern `CLM-<DOMAIN>-<6 digits>`. Ranges in this registry denote consecutive, separately addressable atomic records; they are not aggregate claims. Source citations carry exactly one source role for the field, authority scope, custodian, independence basis, controlled location alias, pinpoint, original/derivative state, snapshot/hash, snapshot/access time, source/effective dates if present, access state, source privacy, redistribution state, and reviewer note.
