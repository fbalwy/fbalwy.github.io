### Research or collaboration context

Include:

- the research question or programme context;
- the scope of the proposed discussion;
- the intended contribution of each participant; and
- the evidence, constraints, or application setting that should be considered.

### Publication, editorial, or scholarly context

Include:

- the publication, subject area, or scholarly activity concerned;
- the purpose of the inquiry;
- the requested contribution or decision; and
- the relevant date or deadline, if one exists.

### Teaching or supervision context

Include:

- the research or study topic;
- relevant academic or technical background;
- the programme or study context; and
- the intended timing.

This category does not imply admission authority, available places, funding, supervision availability, or a response commitment.

### Speaking, professional, technology, or public-sector context

Include:

- the topic or problem context;
- the audience and format;
- the proposed date and location or delivery context, if relevant;
- the expected research or professional contribution; and
- any institutional, confidentiality, or decision constraints that should be understood before a discussion.

This category does not imply acceptance, consulting availability, representation of Taibah University, a partnership, or institutional endorsement.
