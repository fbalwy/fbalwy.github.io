# Faisal Albalwy

Assistant Professor of Cybersecurity and researcher focused on trustworthy digital systems.

Institutional affiliation: [Taibah University (external)](https://www.taibahu.edu.sa/)

## Research and evidence

My work examines how digital systems can protect sensitive data, communicate across technical boundaries, and detect cyber threats. This site connects those questions to canonical publication records and supporting academic context.

- [Explore research](/research)
- [View publications](/publications)

## Research themes

### Privacy-preserving health-data sharing

This work examines consent, access control, encryption, and federated approaches to sharing sensitive health and genomic data.

### Blockchain security and interoperability

This work studies blockchain and smart-contract mechanisms for secure data exchange, system interoperability, and software-defined network security.

### Machine learning for cyber-threat detection

This work examines machine-learning and deep-learning methods for phishing and intrusion detection across networked and Internet of Things environments.
