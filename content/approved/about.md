# About Faisal Albalwy

## Short biography

Faisal Albalwy is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Cybersecurity at Taibah University and a researcher focused on trustworthy digital systems. His publication record spans privacy-preserving health-data sharing, blockchain security and interoperability, and machine learning for cyber-threat detection.

## Extended biography

Faisal Albalwy's research connects cybersecurity and privacy questions with blockchain, smart contracts, encryption, federated learning, and machine-learning methods for threat detection. His work includes studies of consent and policy enforcement for sensitive health data, secure exchange across decentralized and networked systems, and intrusion-detection methods.

The University of Manchester awarded him a PhD on 14 April 2022. His doctoral thesis examined blockchain infrastructure and smart-contract-based consent models for clinical genomics.
